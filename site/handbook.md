The handbook is in [tamnd/singularity-handbook](https://github.com/tamnd/singularity-handbook). Volume 0 introduces the problem, the statements and the map. The later volumes follow the tiers.

A chapter is "checked" only when a person has read it against the Lean statements. Until then, each chapter has the mark "draft".

## The volumes of edition 0.4.76
