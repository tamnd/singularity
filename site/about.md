## How the work is done

A person sets the goals and reviews the results. A program, `sing`, checks the map and computes each status from the Lean build. AI tools write drafts of Lean code, of the handbook and of the records. Lean checks the code, so a proof does not depend on who wrote it. A statement and a text do depend on who wrote them, so each one needs a human review before the project calls it checked.

## Claims

The project does not claim a proof of the open case. It claims a proof of a node only when the Lean build has the proof with the standard axioms. For the open problem it also needs the checks of the [trust page](/trust/) and the review of two outside experts.
