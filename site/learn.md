## The paths

1. **From commutative algebra.** Start with regular local rings, the regular locus and blowups (tier T1). Then read the curves and the surfaces (tier T5).
2. **From characteristic 0.** Start with Hironaka's theorem and the weighted blowups (tier T8). Then read why each step fails in characteristic $p$ (tier T4).
3. **To the open case.** Read the purely inseparable hypersurfaces $z^p + g$, the residual order and the kangaroo points (tier T4). Then read the routes.

The handbook is a draft. Its volumes appear on the [handbook page](/handbook/) when they are ready.
